## 🌠 About the Project

**Neptunians** is an interactive space exploration platform designed to bring the fascinating world of exoplanets closer to everyone.

An exoplanet is a planet that exists outside our solar system, orbiting another star. Thousands of these distant worlds have already been discovered, each with unique characteristics and fascinating possibilities.

Neptunians transforms astronomical data into an engaging and accessible exploration experience. It allows users to discover confirmed exoplanets, investigate their physical properties, compare them with Earth, and explore the possibility of habitable environments beyond our solar system.

By combining real astronomical datasets with interactive visualizations and educational tools, Neptunians aims to make exoplanet research more understandable, accessible, and exciting.

**Our mission:** To make the exploration of distant worlds an experience everyone can participate in.

## ✨ Key Features

### 🔭 1. Exoplanet Discovery Explorer
- Explore a catalog of confirmed exoplanets beyond our solar system.
- Search for planets using their names and available astronomical information.
- Discover fascinating planetary characteristics.
- Access important information about their host stars and discovery history.

### 🔍 2. Advanced Planet Filtering
Explore exoplanets based on different scientific parameters:

- Planetary size and radius.
- Distance from Earth.
- Host star characteristics.
- Discovery year and method.
- Other available physical and orbital properties.

Find planets that match your interests and discover worlds with extraordinary characteristics.

### 🌍 3. Earth Comparison
Discover how distant planets compare with our home planet.

- Compare planetary mass and radius.
- Examine differences in gravitational strength.
- Understand planetary characteristics relative to Earth.
- Explore how different environments could affect human experience.

### ⚖️ 4. Planetary Gravity Calculator
Ever wondered how much you would weigh on another planet?

Neptunians makes this fascinating question interactive by calculating theoretical weight on selected exoplanets using their available mass and radius measurements.

Compare your Earth weight with the equivalent scale reading on another world and understand how gravity differs across planetary environments.

### 🪐 5. Habitable Zone Exploration
Explore the concept of the habitable zone and investigate exoplanets that orbit within potentially suitable distances from their host stars.

Understand why some planetary environments are considered interesting candidates in the search for life beyond Earth.

*Being inside the habitable zone does not guarantee that a planet supports life.*

### ⭐ 6. Planetary System Exploration
Discover distant planetary systems and learn about the planets orbiting their respective host stars.

Explore the diversity of planetary systems beyond our own and understand how their orbital arrangements differ from our solar system.

### 📚 7. Educational Space Discovery
Make astronomy easier to understand through accessible explanations of concepts such as:

- Exoplanets.
- Light-years.
- Planetary mass and radius.
- Surface gravity.
- Orbital periods.
- Habitable zones.
- Host stars and planetary systems.

## 🛰️ Data Source & API Integration

Neptunians uses NASA's Exoplanet Archive as its primary astronomical data source.

**NASA Exoplanet Archive**

https://exoplanetarchive.ipac.caltech.edu/

The archive provides scientific information about confirmed exoplanets, including their physical properties, orbital characteristics, host stars, and discovery details.

The application uses the archive's Table Access Protocol (TAP) interface to retrieve relevant planetary information.

This allows users to explore astronomical discoveries through real scientific datasets rather than relying on manually created planetary records.
